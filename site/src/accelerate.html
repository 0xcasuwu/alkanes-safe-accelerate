What it does, and does not

It clones your transaction and lowers only your own change to pay the higher fee: every input, every output, their order and the OP_RETURN stay byte for byte, so alkanes and runes route exactly as before. Before anything is signed it checks the sat flow and alkane conservation, and simulates the original and the replacement to confirm every output settles identically; after signing it verifies the signed bytes, asks the node (testmempoolaccept) and looks once more for anything new spending the original, and only then broadcasts. "Check" stops before broadcasting.

Not in this POC: adding a fee coin (shrink-change only), and the app's local records of in-flight packages and peg-outs. Rare sats and Atomicals are not indexed anywhere SUBFROST reads.
